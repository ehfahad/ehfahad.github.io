Teaching & Mentoring

I teach through active, student-centered learning, designing each course around what students must be able to do rather than what I must cover. I want students to leave able to judge a claim on their own: what a method assumes, how to build it, where it breaks, and how to evaluate it.

Lecturer, Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Port City International University, Bangladesh 2018–2021

I taught twelve undergraduate computer science courses with classes of 20 to 60 students, and I taught the companion lab in nine of them. I used think-pair-share in lectures and pair programming in labs.

Guest lectures and workshops

Teaching from my research

  • Spring 2026IS 790 · Causal AI Graduate

    Causal representation learning. Why statistical models fail under distribution shift; structural causal models; the independent causal mechanisms and sparse mechanism shift principles; disentangled representations; and case studies of CausalVAE and SCM-VAE, with in-class quizzes. I designed the lecture from recent papers and my own research, and afterward students wrote to me about using the methods in their own work.

  • Mar 2026Explainable AI workshop · iHARP Artificial Intelligence Series

    I led and presented the workshop, developed with Dr. Md Osman Gani: interpretable models and post-hoc explanations; LIME and SHAP; Grad-CAM and attention maps; causal and counterfactual explanations with DiCE; evaluation metrics such as faithfulness and stability; and hands‑on notebooks.
